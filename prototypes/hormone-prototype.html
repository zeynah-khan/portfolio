Brain fog & hormonal change
Cognitive symptoms like brain fog and forgetfulness are commonly linked to hormonal fluctuations, particularly during perimenopause and with conditions like PCOS and PMDD — but they're rarely discussed alongside physical symptoms. Logging when they happen can help you notice patterns worth raising with a healthcare provider, without framing it as something being "wrong."
Select a focus and goals, then log symptoms to see Home and Insights adapt — the way FR3 and FR4 describe.
